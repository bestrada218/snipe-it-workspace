# Activity and history

Where to see what happened to each item and how to adjust the activity report.

## Each asset's history

Every asset page has a **history** tab with everything that happened to it: creation, check-outs and check-ins, data changes, audits, maintenances, shipments, damage reports, duplicate approvals, return notes and tag changes.

Each data change is shown in a readable way in **Change detail**: the field, the old value and the new one. When an old record did not keep the value, it says "Old value not recorded".

## The activity report

[Reports → Activity Report](route:reports.activity) shows everything that happens in the system, for every person and item, with filters and search.

### Adjusting the columns

Each person can adjust their view, and it is saved for next time:

- **Move a column**: drag the dots in its header.
- **Change the width**: drag the header's edge.
- **Choose columns**: show or hide them with the table's columns button. Your profile also links here with **Configure activity report columns**.
- **Reset to default view** puts it back as it was.

## Exporting

Lists and reports have buttons above each table to **export** to CSV or Excel and to print.
