# Damage reports

What is asked when an asset moves to a damaged status and where it is recorded.

## When it shows up

The administrator chooses which statuses mean "damaged" (e.g. *Broken* or *Damaged*). When someone moves an asset to one of them, **the form requires a damage report** before saving.

Assets in those statuses **cannot be assigned** to anyone.

## What to fill in

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **What is damaged** | A description in English, e.g. "Screen cracked and does not power on after the drop". Required. |
| **Can it be repaired?** | Yes / No, it cannot be repaired / Not known yet. |
| **Was a repair attempted?** | Yes or No. |
| **What was tried and what happened** | When a repair was tried, in English: who repaired it, when and the result. |

## Where it is kept

The report is saved in the **asset history** as "Damage reported", with the status change, the date and who made it. Even if the asset is repaired later, the record of what happened stays.

<!-- admin-only -->

## Setting up damage statuses

In **Settings → General**, field **Statuses that require a damage report**, a superadmin chooses one or more statuses. Choose statuses that mean broken or damaged equipment that can no longer be assigned.
