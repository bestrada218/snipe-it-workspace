# Mandatory replacement

How to set the date an asset must be replaced by and how you are warned before it is due.

## What it is for

Some equipment must be replaced after a certain time (warranty, policy or wear). Mandatory replacement keeps that **deadline** on each asset and warns ahead of time, so the purchase can be planned.

## Setting the date on an asset

When creating or editing an asset, in the **Mandatory replacement** block:

1. Tick **This asset requires mandatory replacement**.
2. In **How to set the deadline** choose:
   - **Years**: how many **years since the asset was registered**. The date is calculated and shown below.
   - **Exact date**: pick the **replacement deadline** (it must be in the future).
3. The **time remaining** is shown below. Save.

## Alerts

Assets with a deadline appear in [Tracking → Mandatory replacement alerts](route:hardware.replacement-alerts), grouped by how much time is left:

| Window | Includes |
| --- | --- |
| **Overdue** | The date has passed. |
| **Today or tomorrow** | Due in 0 or 1 day. |
| **Up to 15 days** | |
| **Up to 1 month** | |
| **Up to 3 months** | |

Each asset appears once, in the nearest window. **View replacement schedule** shows every scheduled one.

The number next to the menu entry counts those needing attention. On the dashboard, **Needs attention** shows overdue ones and those due within 15 days.

## What to do as the date gets close

1. Check the tag and who holds it.
2. Buy or prepare the replacement.
3. Hand over the new equipment (remotely, as a [shipment](help:shipments)) and take back the old one, for example with a [return](help:returns) for a change.
4. On the old asset, remove the mandatory replacement or change its status as appropriate.
